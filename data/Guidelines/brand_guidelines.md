# Brand Guidelines - ScentFlow AI

## 1. Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** ScentFlow

**Industry:** Luxury Fragrance

**Brand Positioning:**
ScentFlow is a luxury fragrance house that blends craftsmanship, elegance, and individuality to create distinctive fragrance experiences.

**Vision:**
To create unforgettable fragrance experiences where every scent tells a unique story.

**Core Values:**
- Elegance
- Craftsmanship
- Authenticity
- Excellence
- Emotional Connection

## 2. Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

### Brand Personality:

ScentFlow communicates as a luxury fragrance expert:

- Expert and knowledgeable
- Elegant and confident
- Inspiring and emotionally aware


### Communication Style:

The brand communication should be:

**Sophisticated:**
Uses refined language that reflects luxury and craftsmanship.

**Emotional:**
Connects fragrances with memories, feelings, and personal identity.

**Clear:**
Uses simple and elegant expressions that are easy to understand.


### The brand should avoid:

- Casual language
- Aggressive sales messages
- Exaggerated claims
- Discount-focused communication


## 3. Messaging & Copywriting Rules

### 3.1 Approved Vocabulary (Do's)

ScentFlow content should use refined vocabulary that reflects luxury, fragrance expertise, and emotional connection.

### Luxury & Identity:

Use words that communicate elegance, exclusivity, and a premium identity:

- Signature scent
- Sophisticated
- Refined
- Exclusive
- Crafted
- Timeless elegance


### Fragrance Experience:

Use fragrance-related terminology to describe the scent journey and composition:

- Notes
- Scent profile
- Essence
- Harmony
- Composition
- Olfactory journey


### Emotional Connection:

Use expressions that connect fragrances with personal identity, emotions, and memories:

- Elevate your presence
- Express your identity
- Create lasting memories
- Unveil your signature scent


---

### 3.2 Prohibited Vocabulary & Practices (Don'ts)

Avoid language that reduces the luxury perception of the brand or creates unsupported claims.


### Cheap Promotional Language:

Avoid:

- Cheap
- Lowest price
- Huge discount
- Hurry before it ends
- Buy now


### Unverified Claims:

Avoid:

- Guaranteed attraction
- Cures stress
- Improves health
- Lasts forever


### Aggressive Marketing Style:

Avoid:

- Excessive urgency
- Over-promising
- Excessive use of exclamation marks


# 4. Channel-Specific Content Guidelines

The content style should be adapted based on the requirements and audience behavior of each platform.

---

# 4.1 Instagram

## Purpose:

Build an emotional connection with the target audience and showcase the fragrance story through elegant and engaging content.

---

## Content Structure:

1. **Marketing Message / Hook**
   
   A short and memorable opening message that captures the audience's attention and reflects the fragrance identity.

2. **Fragrance Story**

   Describe the inspiration, feeling, and experience behind the fragrance.

3. **Soft & Elegant CTA**

   Encourage engagement or discovery without using aggressive sales language.

**Example:**
"Your fragrance completes your story."

---

## Preferred Length:

30-60 words.

---

## Hashtags:

- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags.
- Avoid unrelated trending hashtags.

---

## Emojis:

Use minimal and elegant emojis:

✨ 🖤 🌿 🤍


- Use 1-2 emojis maximum.
- Avoid excessive emoji usage.
